Access

How your company
gets access

The app is for our partners. You get access once we agree on a partnership.

01

Get in touch

Call us or send a message. Tell us how many vehicles you have, at which addresses and what you need.

02

We agree the terms

Together we agree on services, price, partner benefits and a visit schedule, for one or more locations.

03

You get access

After we agree, your company gets access to the Mojsilov business app. From then on you book yourself, whenever you need.

Booking

Booked in three
or four clicks

The employee booking does not need to call or wait for a reply. Once the booking is confirmed, we take care of the rest.

01

Who the booking is for

Choose whether the booking is for a company vehicle or for a guest at your property.

02

Address and service

Choose the agreed address and the service you need.

03

Day and time

Choose an available slot and leave a note for the team if needed, for example where the car is parked.

04

We come to your address

Our team arrives at the agreed time with full equipment and our own water and power. When we finish, you get before and after photos of the vehicle.

Other ways

Prefer
the phone?

The app is just one option. You and your guests choose whatever suits you best.

24/7 call center

Our call center is available 24 hours a day, every day, for booking, information about services and support with an existing appointment.

Your staff call for the guest

When a guest tells you they want their car cleaned, you call us and pass on their request, address and preferred time. We take over the communication with the guest from there.

The guest calls us directly

Just give the guest our number. We agree the service, the car's location and the arrival time with them, and handle everything else.
