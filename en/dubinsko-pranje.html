What makes the difference

Why deep cleaning
is not an ordinary wash

The difference is depth

A car wash cleans what you can see; deep cleaning removes what you cannot. With a Kärcher extraction vacuum and a steam cleaner we pull dirt out from deep in the fabric, disinfect the interior and reach places an ordinary wash never gets to.

The difference in time and value

The treatment takes 4 to 5 hours because every part of the car is treated separately, from the headliner and seats to the rims and trunk. Regular professional care preserves the interior and paint and directly affects the resale value. It is not a cost but an investment in what your car is worth.
