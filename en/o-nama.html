Our story

The same principle since day one

We built Mojsilov Detailing step by step, from humble beginnings to a premium mobile service across Belgrade. Through every stage one principle stayed the same: treating every car as if it were our own.

Our story

How we got here

Every stage built the same foundation: uncompromising quality and relationships built for the long term.

Stage 01

The beginning

We started with basic equipment and a strong drive to make every result flawless. Despite modest conditions, our commitment and attention to detail were complete from day one.

Stage 02

First steps forward

Next came an investment in professional equipment. That is when our first working standards were set: a more precise process and a service warranty.

Stage 03

Going professional

Our own service vehicle gave us full independence in the field, with no borrowing and no waiting. With a social media presence and a wider range of services, the business grew into a serious professional operation.

Stage 04

Premium standard today

Today we have more than 500 jobs done and 130+ positive reviews behind us, without a single unhappy customer. The same principle as on day one, now with premium equipment and a warranty of up to 24 months.
