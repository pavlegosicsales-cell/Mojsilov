Is it worth it

Why headlight restoration
is not a small thing

Better visibility at night

Cloudy and yellowed headlights light the road poorly, so the driver notices pedestrians, obstacles and other hazards later. Research by the AAA (American Automobile Association) found that badly worn headlights can give only about 22% of the light of new ones. Restoration brings back the clarity of the headlights, improves visibility and makes night driving safer.

Passing the vehicle inspection

The vehicle inspection checks the condition and beam of the main headlights. Cloudy and yellowed headlights let less light through, so a car can fail the inspection if the headlights do not meet the requirements. Restoration brings back their clarity and improves the chance of passing, provided the rest of the lighting works properly.

A better-looking car

Cloudy and yellowed headlights make a car look older and neglected, even when the rest of it is tidy. After restoration the headlights regain their shine and clarity, and the front of the car looks cleaner, newer and more attractive.

Cheaper than replacement

A headlight that is cloudy and yellowed on the outside can often be restored without buying a new one. On some cars, replacing the whole headlight can cost many times more than professional restoration.

Our process

How we bring back the shine
to your headlights

Step 01

Preparation and sanding

We carefully prepare the headlight by wet sanding, with grits from P240 to P3000, in 8 to 10 stages depending on the condition of the headlight. This step removes oxidation and fine scratches, and it is the foundation of a lasting result.

Step 02

Three-stage polishing

We polish the headlights with a dual-action polisher in three stages (Heavy Cut, Medium Cut and Finish) with professional abrasive compounds. A dual-action polisher reduces heat on the plastic and minimizes the risk of holograms.

Step 03

Protection (optional)

For longer durability we apply a professional ceramic coating with a UV filter. It protects the headlight from sunlight and the elements and makes the result last longer. With ceramic protection the warranty is extended to 24 months.
